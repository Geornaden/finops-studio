How Bank ISO 20022 Status Reporting Works

Level 1: GrpStsSyntax & CtrlSum

1. Group Header Status (OrgnlGrpInfAndSts)

First-stage technical acknowledgment (ACTC, PART, or RJCT). Whole-file RJCT here usually indicates XSD schema violations (FF01), duplicate MsgId (DU01), or cent-sum mismatch (AM09).

Level 2: PmtInfStsDebtor Account

2. Payment Batch Status (OrgnlPmtInfAndSts)

Validates the originating debtor account, requested execution date (DT01), SEPA scheme eligibility, and batch-level authorization limits (AM04 / AG01).

Level 3: TxInfAndStsVoP & 2026 BE04

3. Individual Transaction Exceptions

Pinpoints single failed transfers by OrgnlEndToEndId—such as 2026 EPC missing structured creditor town/country (BE04), Verification of Payee name mismatch (BE01), or closed beneficiary IBAN (AC04).
