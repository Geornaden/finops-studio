Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my ERP reject a manually edited BAI2 file on Record 49 or Record 99?

Treasury ERPs (NetSuite Automated Cash Application, SAP S/4HANA Electronic Bank Statement, Kyriba, Workday) validate both the unsigned integer control total (which includes Record 03 status/summary balances PLUS Record 16 transaction amounts) and the exact physical line count (counting every 88 continuation line). Removing or editing a single line without recalculating 49, 98, and 99 triggers an immediate trailer checksum rejection.
