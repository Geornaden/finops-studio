📖 New Zealand Construction Contracts Act (CCA) 2023 Retention Money Requirements

Statutory Obligation Legal Requirement (CCA 2023 Amendment) Compliance Risk & Penalties
Mandatory Trust Status Retention money withheld under a commercial construction contract is deemed to be held on trust as soon as it is withheld from a payment claim. Strict liability offence for failing to hold retention money properly. Maximum penalty of $200,000 for companies and $50,000 for directors.
Separate Bank Account Retention funds must be held in a designated trust account with a registered NZ bank (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Westpac, Kiwibank) or covered by a qualifying financial instrument. Funds cannot be commingled with head contractor operating cash flow or used as working capital under any circumstance.
Accounting Records & Inspection Head contractors must maintain proper accounting ledger records identifying each subcontractor, retention amounts withheld, and trust deposits. Subcontractors have a statutory right to inspect retention records quarterly at zero charge.
Section 21 Payment Schedules Payers must issue a payment schedule within statutory timeframes specifying scheduled amount, retention deductions, and net payable. Failure to provide a schedule triggers the full claimed amount becoming a statutory debt due immediately.
