Why did Deutsche Bank, BNP Paribas, ING, or Revolut reject our SEPA XML batch with code BE04?

Under the 2026 European Payments Council (EPC) rulebook update, unstructured <AdrLine> tags inside legacy pain.001.001.03 and pain.008.001.02 files trigger compliance errors when <Ctry> and <TwnNm> are missing. Switching to pain.001.001.09 (Credit Transfers) and pain.008.001.08 (Direct Debits) with structured Town Name and ISO Country elements resolves BE04 rejections immediately.

What else causes bulk SEPA batch rejections?

Floating-point rounding mismatches in <CtrlSum> (Code AM09), invalid IBAN MOD-97 check digits (Code AC01), and unescaped characters like & or umlauts (Code FF01). FinOps Studio checks every IBAN mathematically and computes integer-cent sums automatically.
