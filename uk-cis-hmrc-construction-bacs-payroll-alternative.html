📖 Technical Guide: BACS Standard 18 Record Layout for UK CIS Contractors

The BACS Standard 18 format is a fixed-width 100-byte specification with strict character positioning:

Cols Length Field Name Technical Value & Format
01 - 06 6 Destination Sort Subcontractor bank sort code (numeric, 6 digits)
07 - 14 8 Destination Account Subcontractor bank account number (numeric, 8 digits)
15 - 15 1 Account Type 0 (Standard UK current or commercial account)
16 - 17 2 Transaction Code 99 for Subcontractor Credit, 17 for Contractor CONTRA Debit
18 - 23 6 Originating Sort Contractor corporate bank sort code (numeric, 6 digits)
24 - 31 8 Originating Account Contractor corporate account number (numeric, 8 digits)
32 - 35 4 Free Format / Filler 0000
36 - 46 11 Amount in Pence Exact Net Payable amount in pence, right-aligned, zero-padded (e.g. 00000325000)
47 - 64 18 Originating Account Name Contractor business name, uppercase, left-aligned, space-padded to 18 chars
65 - 82 18 Reference / Narration Subbie UTR, invoice, or CIS reference, left-aligned, space-padded to 18 chars
83 - 100 18 Destination Account Name Subcontractor name, uppercase, left-aligned, space-padded to 18 chars
