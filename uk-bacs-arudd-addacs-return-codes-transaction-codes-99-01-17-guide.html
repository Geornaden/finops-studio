Frequently Asked UK BACS & Direct Debit Engineering Questions

      

        
What is the difference between BACS Transaction Codes 99, 01, 17, 18, and 19?

        
99 is a Bacs Direct Credit (payroll, supplier AP, HMRC/pension/expense payouts). Codes 01, 17, 18, and 19 are Direct Debit collections: 01 initiates the first collection on a new Direct Debit Instruction, 17 collects ongoing recurring instalments, 18 re-presents an unpaid Direct Debit within 30 days after notifying the payer, and 19 collects the final payment closing a mandate.

      

      

        
Why does Barclays.net, Lloyds Commercial, or NatWest Bankline reject a Standard 18 file on upload?

        
Over 90% of Bacstel-IP upload rejections come from four formatting bugs: (1) lines that are 99 or 101 bytes instead of exact 100 ASCII characters, (2) hyphens left inside 6-digit UK Sort Codes (20-45-45 instead of 204545), (3) non-BACS punctuation or lowercase characters in columns 47–100, or (4) a CONTRA / UTL1 penny total mismatch. Our UK BACS Standard 18 Generator API sanitizes and mathematically verifies every field automatically.
