Frequently Asked Australian `.ABA` Direct Entry Questions

      

        
Should I use Transaction Code 50 or 53 for Australian payroll vs contractor invoices?

        
Use 53 (Pay) for employee wages and salaries (TFN payroll runs), and 50 (Externally Initiated Credit) for ABN supplier invoices, contractor bills, superannuation clearing house transfers, and expense reimbursements.

      

      

        
What APCA User ID (`apcaUserId`) do I use if my business uploads via NetBank or CommBiz web banking?

        
Commonwealth Bank NetBank business uploads commonly accept generic APCA ID 301500, whereas Direct Entry facility holders (CommBiz DE, NAB Connect DE, Westpac Corporate Online, ANZ Transactive) enter their bank-issued 6-digit APCA Direct Entry User ID in columns 57–62 of Record 0.
